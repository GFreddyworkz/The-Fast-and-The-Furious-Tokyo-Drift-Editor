How to use it

  1. In PPSSPP, turn savedata encryption off, then find your game’s SECURE.BIN (the decrypted save).
  2. Open it here, pick a car at the top, and make your changes on any tab. Every change is listed under Changes made.
  3. Press Download patched save (zip), extract the new SECURE.BIN over the old one, and load the game.

Always keep a copy of your original save. Everything runs in your browser, nothing is uploaded.

Good to know

  • The shop only offers parts a car is allowed to have. Parts marked with a warning load fine, but the shop may remove them when you buy something else.
  • Many cars are not allowed any engine swap. On those, the game may remove the swapped engine. The Engine tab warns you and suggests a swap the car accepts.
  • The Engine curve panel only works on a car that already has a swapped-in engine. A car on its own stock engine has nothing in the save to edit.
  • Very large values (around a million and up) can turn the screen black while the game keeps running. Nothing stops you from typing them, but if it happens, load an earlier save and use smaller numbers.
  • Body paint only changes the car’s main colour, not its vinyls. The game’s shop may show a different colour name until you repaint in game.
  • Not available yet: vinyl editing, wins and losses.
